# Sample Prompts for Critical Literary Text Analyst

These prompts are designed to test the LLM skill for analysing Hindi poetry.

## 1. Ecological Elements

Identify the ecological elements in the supplied passage from *Kamayani*. Categorise them into flora, fauna, water, landforms, and natural processes.

## 2. Symbolic Interpretation

Explain the symbolic significance of water and mountains in the supplied Hindi poetic passage. Support your interpretation with textual evidence.

## 3. Human–Nature Relationship

Analyse how the supplied passage represents the relationship between human beings and nature.

## 4. Cultural Analysis

Identify cultural, philosophical, and mythological references in the passage. Explain their significance using textual evidence.

## 5. Critical Literary Reading

Provide a critical interpretation of the supplied passage, discussing its ecological imagery, symbolism, cultural context, and limitations.
